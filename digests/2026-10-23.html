Why this matters to me

Today's through-line is the slow structural squeeze on international student recruitment, and it is now a global pattern, not a Canadian anomaly. For a practice selling into Canadian higher ed, this is the demand-side reality your enrollment clients are living — the funnel is shrinking everywhere at once, which raises the premium on yield, differentiation, and AI-assisted recruitment efficiency. On the real estate side, the HousingWire piece on leading-indicator dashboards is a clean illustration of the operational-analytics thesis you can carry into brokerage conversations. Watch the Carney-Turkey file too: a Candu nuclear bid and the midterm-interference denials signal a federal government leaning hard into trade diplomacy while US relations stay tense. Nothing today demands action, but the enrollment-contraction story is your thought-leadership lane — the institutions that invest in smarter, governed recruitment now will separate from the ones cutting marketing in a panic.
